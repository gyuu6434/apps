Privacy Policy

For all iOS apps published on the App Store by Seonggyu Kim. Last updated: 2026-11-13

Summary

Our apps do not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything you do in them happens on your device.

Your photos, videos, and files

When an app needs a photo, video, or document, you choose it with the system picker, so the app can only see the items you pick. They are processed entirely on your device and never uploaded to any server. Working copies are deleted when you are done with them.

When an app saves a result to your photo library, it asks for permission to add photos. It can add images but cannot read or browse your library.

If an app ever needs another permission, such as the camera, it asks first and uses that access only on your device for the task you started.

Data we collect

None. Our apps have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and they do not connect to our servers.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information. The number of free uses left is stored only on your device.

Children

Our apps do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
