Free uses and the one-time unlock

Each app lets you try its main feature a few times for free. A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited use. There is no subscription.

I bought the unlock on another iPhone. How do I get it back?

Open the app's upgrade screen and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I want a refund

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The app can't save to Photos

Open Settings, find the app (under Apps on iOS 18 and later), tap Photos, and allow adding photos.

Privacy

Your files never leave your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
